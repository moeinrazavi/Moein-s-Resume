\noindent Dear Professor Mostafavi,

I am writing to express my strong interest in the Post-Doctoral Research Scientist position. As a \textbf{Texas A\&M Ph.D. graduate} (4.0 GPA) with \textbf{470+ citations} across 16 publications and a current \textbf{Senior AI/ML Engineer at Ford} building production-scale multi-agent AI and transformer systems, I am eager to bring my full expertise in AI, multi-modal learning, and large-scale data systems to the mission of \textbf{disrupting disasters with AI}. I am in the US and available immediately.

\noindent\textbf{\color{headerblue}Direct Alignment with Lab Priorities.}\enspace
My research and industry work map directly onto the technical areas your lab prioritizes:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.4cm, itemsep=0.12em, topsep=0.1em, parsep=0em, label={\color{headerblue}\textbullet}]
  \item \textbf{Reasoning Models \& Agentic AI / Tabular RAG:} At Ford, I architected a \textbf{production multi-agent system} (LangChain, LangGraph) with chain-of-thought reasoning, \textbf{RAG over tabular data} in BigQuery, and vector databases (FAISS, ChromaDB)---an SQL Agent, Document Agent, and LLM Decider serving 10 US states. This architecture transfers directly to building disaster reasoning agents over infrastructure inventories, damage assessments, and census data.

  \item \textbf{Multi-Modal Learning:} My Ph.D. centered on multi-modal sensor fusion. I designed a \textbf{dual-branch transformer} with cross-attention fusion achieving \textbf{AUC 0.993} for wearable stress detection, and built \textbf{OpenSync} (31 citations)---an open-source platform synchronizing EEG, eye-tracking, and biosensor streams in real time. This extends naturally to fusing satellite imagery, social media, sensor networks, and geospatial data for disaster assessment.

  \item \textbf{Spatial ML \& Vision-Language Models:} I co-authored a \textbf{county-level COVID-19 risk study} (9 citations) combining spatial, demographic, and socioeconomic features with ML, and deployed a \textbf{computer vision system} for pandemic safety monitoring at 98\% accuracy over 50K+ daily interactions (76 citations). My combined CV and NLP depth positions me to develop vision-language pipelines for post-disaster damage assessment.

  \item \textbf{Graph Foundation Models:} My experience with graph-structured reasoning in agentic AI, network-level data pipelines at scale (10+ TB), and transformer architectures provides a strong foundation for developing GNNs and graph foundation models to analyze interdependent infrastructure networks and predict cascading failures.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{-0.1em}

\noindent\textbf{\color{headerblue}Research Vision.}\enspace
I envision two high-impact contributions: \textbf{(1)} building an \textbf{agentic ``disaster co-pilot''}---a multi-agent system that ingests real-time multi-modal data (satellite imagery, IoT sensors, social media, infrastructure status) and provides chain-of-thought reasoning with explainable recommendations to emergency managers; and \textbf{(2)} developing \textbf{multi-modal foundation models for urban resilience} that jointly learn from tabular infrastructure data, geospatial imagery, and textual incident reports, enabling few-shot transfer across disaster types.

As a proud Aggie, I would be honored to return to Texas A\&M and dedicate my next chapter to building cutting-edge AI solutions with real impact on community resilience.
